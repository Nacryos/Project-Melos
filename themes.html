About themes and search units

Nature categories use existing aesthetic annotations. Their paintings are visual interpretations. “Explore ideas” starts a thematic search; its prompts are not certified literary classifications.

Smaller units are exact source spans within retrieved passages. For a query, the local semantic model can rank a bounded sample of these spans on demand. There is no independently stored index of child embeddings. If that model is unavailable, results retain literal or annotation evidence and parent relevance; each search reports its method below.

Stanzas are typographic blocks, and punctuation determines sentences and phrases; these divisions are not editorial certifications.
