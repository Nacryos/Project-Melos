    About the chronology & images

    Dates identify a sourced birth or period of activity, where available. They are biographical claims, not dates of composition. Authors without a supported date follow the chronology alphabetically. Source details and uncertainty accompany each author.

    Portraits are historical depictions, attributions or later imaginings, not verified likenesses. Images are cropped for this presentation; original files and image credits are retained. Counts refer to indexed source records, including separate editions and translations, rather than unique poems.
