# Second-pass source discovery audit

Audited 2026-09-30 against `docs/augmentation-contract.md`, `docs/corpus-contract.md`, and the data-extraction skill. This file records source-level decisions only. `PASS` authorizes scoped staging subject to exact-file notices and downstream text/claim audits; `WARN` allows bounded staging with the stated caveat; `FAIL` rejects the proposed URL or asserted use. It does not accept any extracted record.

## Pinned sources available for reuse

The independently audited first-pass decisions in [source-audit.md](source-audit.md) remain applicable to the same pinned files and scopes. Relevant verdicts: Perseus canonical-greekLit, canonical-latinLit, lexica, and treebank_data are conditional on per-file notices; Open Greek Corpus requires underlying edition/source attribution and has unresolved editor credits in some rows; DCC Sappho and Digital Sappho are related modern editorial witnesses; Scaife atlas-data-prep dictionaries and Homerica Autenrieth have unresolved data reuse rights. New files or editions require file-level review. A mirrored Perseus, DCC, or First1KGreek row does not become an independent witness by appearing in Open Greek Corpus or Scaife.

### Hephaestion upstream control files

The three OGC Hephaestion files have exact First1KGreek counterparts at pinned commit `8ee111eb44ecef4120c844e10749178d95d1f30c`: [Enchiridion de metris](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenGreekAndLatin/First1KGreek/8ee111eb44ecef4120c844e10749178d95d1f30c/data/tlg1402/tlg001/tlg1402.tlg001.1st1K-grc1.xml), [Metrica introductio](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenGreekAndLatin/First1KGreek/8ee111eb44ecef4120c844e10749178d95d1f30c/data/tlg1402/tlg002/tlg1402.tlg002.1st1K-grc1.xml), and [De poematis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenGreekAndLatin/First1KGreek/8ee111eb44ecef4120c844e10749178d95d1f30c/data/tlg1402/tlg003/tlg1402.tlg003.1st1K-grc1.xml). All three resolve and contain explicit CC BY-SA 4.0 TEI `<availability>` plus a `sourceDesc` for Consbruch's 1906 Teubner edition and individual digital credits. The first file's title statement spells the editor *Corsbruch* while its source description says *Consbruch*; retain the original header and flag this discrepancy. Its section 1.3 supplies a cited Alcman reference in `<bibl>` where the OGC transformed line shows empty parentheses. Quote/citation claims need TEI-level verification, not reconstructed OGC placeholders.

## Pending source decisions

Collectors must provide exact source and rights URLs, a named work or file, the intended content type, and underlying edition/source family before ingestion. Modern editions, Loeb content, scans, and articles go to `p2_edition_audit`. Unknown rights must be recorded as unknown rather than converted into either permission or a prohibition. Any conditional PASS still requires raw artifacts, hashes, source-specific attribution, and a later independent output audit.
