# Open sources for Ancient Greek: survey and first ingestion (2026-10-09)

Status: research and staging only. Nothing here is deployed or wired into `backend/`. New data lives in
`data/open/<name>/`, each folder with a `manifest.json` (source URL, pinned commit/revision or scan id, licence quoted
verbatim, attribution, sha256 of inputs and outputs, counts, date, User-Agent `Melos/1.0 (+https://greeklyric.com)`).
Each folder is rebuilt by `scripts/ingest_open_<name>.py`. The full ranked table at the end is generated by
`scripts/build_open_sources_survey.py` from the survey rows in `data/open/survey-2026-10-09/`.

Why: the owner found Logeion himself and asked for a harder search. Logeion is still off limits (no licence or terms,
private API key; see `docs/plans/logeion-and-form-tables.md`). Everything open that it shows can be had from the
original source, and this survey goes much wider than lexica.

## Method

- Three survey passes (texts/papyri/commentaries/translations; lexica/morphology/treebanks/NLP models; metre/dating/
  prosopography/semantics), with web searches in English, German, French, Italian and Spanish over GitHub, Zenodo,
  HuggingFace, figshare, CLARIN, Internet Archive, HathiTrust (full view only), Wikisource, Gallica and Google Books.
  155 rows. Licences were quoted from the fetched licence/README/model card/metadata page. Rows not verified
  that way say so in their licence cell.
- No pirated uploads were used. Some Internet Archive uploads look unauthorised, such as Digital Library of India
  copies of Edmonds' *Elegy and Iambus* and Spanish *Líricos griegos* translations; they were noted and not used. No
  logins or paywalls were bypassed. TLG and Logeion terms were respected.
- Known slip: one automated HathiTrust full-text search was made before its robots.txt was read (robots.txt forbids
  automated search). The search was not repeated; Internet Archive full-text search was used instead.
- Basecamp SSH was blocked mid-session by a Tailscale browser check. Raw archives already copied there are listed
  below. Everything else was processed on the laptop.

## Not open, or blocked

- **Copyright:** the TLG Canon and texts ("you may not download them"), Slater's *Lexicon to Pindar*, the LSJ
  Supplements, Powell, Frisk, Chantraine, Beekes, Montanari, Brill and the Cambridge Greek Lexicon.
- **Non-commercial or copyright:** West, Dale and Maas on metre; Chicago Homer; BabelNet; the Pedecerto/MQDQ data.
- **Non-commercial training data:** OdyCy, greCy and Stanza grc are trained on NC treebanks. The status of the model
  weights is unresolved, so check before relying on them publicly.
